Which dance floor is right for my event?+

Oak Wood brings a classic feel to indoor and outdoor events. Infinity pairs mirrors and LEDs for an indoor experience. Slate White offers a clean, versatile look. Share your venue and vision with the team to choose the best fit.

Can the floors be used outdoors?+

Oak Wood is available for indoor and outdoor use. Infinity is for indoor use only. Tell us about your venue and ground surface so the team can confirm the right setup for your event.

Can I book a DJ as well?+

Yes. Elite Surfaces offers a network of professional DJs for weddings, corporate events, and private celebrations. Add DJ services to your inquiry and share the kind of music you have in mind.

What information do you need for a quote?+

Your event date, location, guest count, preferred floor, and the surface it will sit on. Include any venue requirements or questions so the team can help plan your setup.
